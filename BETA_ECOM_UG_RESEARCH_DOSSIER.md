## 3. Commercial Operations & Brand Portfolio

BETA E-Com UG operates simultaneously as a **specialist e-commerce operating company**, a **digital commerce solution provider**, and a **merchant across distinct verticals**:

### 1. Tuning-Couture (`tuning-couture.de`)
* **Role of BETA E-Com UG**: Official shop operator and legal commercial entity for e-commerce transactions and copyright.
* **Specialization**:
  * Automotive culture streetwear, enthusiast apparel, and lifestyle merchandise.
  * Print magazine subscriptions and individual issues (*Eurotuner*, *BMW Power*, *Mercedes Tuner*, *Audi Scene*, *Maximum Tuner*, *Cars & Stripes*, *WOB Klassik*).
  * High-grade annual automotive calendars and posters.
  * **Event Ticketing Engine**: Primary ticketing distributor for major automotive enthusiast events, notably **MO's Garage Drag Day** / **DRAG DAY** at Meinerzhagen Airfield (*Flugplatz Meinerzhagen*) for racer and visitor tickets.

### 2. BestofCandy (`bestofcandy.de` / *Bestofcandy BETA E-COM UG*)
* **Role of BETA E-Com UG**: Legal operator of the retail brand and commercial infrastructure.
* **Specialization**:
  * Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) online shop and local retail storefront in Bergisch Gladbach Herkenrath (*Braunsberger Feld 13c*).
  * Specializes in imported authentic American confectionery, snacks, soft drinks, cereals, and BBQ sauces (e.g., Reese's, Hershey's, Pop-Tarts, Cheetos, Sweet Baby Ray's).
  * Inventory of over 200 authentic US original products.
  * Platform Technology: Gambio E-Commerce Engine.

### 3. Custom Technical E-Commerce & Web Applications
* **Client Implementation**: Development of specialized digital e-commerce tools, notably interactive online product configurators.
  * *Reference*: Technical realization of the configurator for **GEBHARDT Automotive GmbH** (*"Konfigurator Umsetzung: BETA E-COM UG"*).
* **Services Provided**:
  * Custom shop development and frontend/backend integration.
  * Shop rental and turnkey e-commerce setups for business partners.
  * Event ticketing and secure checkout systems.
